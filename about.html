About Indo Kids Academy

Indo Kids Academy Senior Secondary School is located at 33 & 34, Subash Colony, Kamla Nehru Nagar, Ajmer Road, Jaipur, Rajasthan.

The school provides education from Nursery through Class 12 and follows the Rajasthan Board of Secondary Education (RBSE) curriculum as specified by the school.

Our school aims to provide students with a balanced educational experience that supports academic learning as well as personal growth, discipline, confidence and responsible citizenship.

We believe every child has unique abilities. Our goal is to encourage students to discover their strengths and develop a positive approach towards learning.
